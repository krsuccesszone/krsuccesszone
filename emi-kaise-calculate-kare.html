EMI kaise calculate karein — Easy Modern Guide

✍️ KR Success Zone 📅 May 2026 🕐 5 min read

Loan lena ek badi zimmedari hai — aur agar aapko pehle se pata ho ki har mahine kitna dena hoga, toh decision lena bahut aasaan ho jaata hai. Aaj hum EMI ka poora khel samjhenge — formula se lekar real-life examples tak.

Jab bhi hum koi bada kharcha karte hain — ghar, gaadi, ya phone — bank se loan lete hain. Aur bank kehta hai: "Theek hai, paise de do, par har mahine thoda thoda lauta do." Yahi hoti hai EMI — Equated Monthly Instalment.

Lekin aksar log loan le lete hain bina yeh jaane ki asli mein kitna dena padega. Fir ek din statement dekhte hain aur soch mein pad jaate hain — "Itna kaise ho gaya?" Toh aaj hum yeh raaz khol rahe hain.

EMI hoti kya hai?

EMI ek fixed monthly payment hai jo aap loan lene ke baad bank ko dete ho — har mahine, ek fixed tarikh par, ek fixed amount. Isme do cheezein hoti hain:

Pehla — Principal yaani woh paise jo aapne actually udhare the. Doosra — Interest yaani bank ka khaana-paani, matlab unka munafa.

Shuruaat mein aapki EMI ka zyada hissa interest mein jaata hai, aur dhire dhire principal ghatta hai. Yahi banking ka "magic" hai — jo actually aapke khilaf kaam karta hai agar aap jaldi repay nahi karte.

EMI calculate karne ka formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]
P = Principal  |  r = Monthly interest rate  |  n = Number of months

Dekhne mein daraaona lagta hai, lekin ek baar example se samajh lo — phir aapko koi calculator bhi poochh nahi sakta!

Matlab ek ₹8 Lakh ki gaadi ke liye aap total ₹9.96 Lakh doge — yaani almost ₹2 Lakh extra sirf interest mein. Isliye gaadi kharidne se pehle hamesha EMI calculator chalaana chahiye!

EMI ko affect karne wale 3 factors

1. Interest Rate kam ho toh?

Wahi ₹30 Lakh home loan agar 7.5% par mile toh EMI ₹24,167 hogi — yani ₹1,868 har mahine ki bachat. 20 saal mein yeh ₹4.4 Lakh ki bachat ban jaati hai!

2. Tenure zyada karo toh?

Tenure badhane se EMI toh kam hoti hai, lekin total interest bahut zyada ho jaata hai. 20 saal ka loan 25 saal karo — EMI ₹2,000 kam hogi, par 5 saal zyada interest doge!

3. Down payment zyada karo toh?

Loan amount hi kam ho jaye toh EMI apne aap kam ho jaati hai. Agar ₹30 Lakh ki jagah ₹25 Lakh loan lo, toh EMI mein ₹4,300+ ki monthly bachat hogi.

Ek zaroori baat — sirf EMI mat dekho. Hamesha Total Cost of Loan calculate karo. ₹500 ki EMI ka fark lagta hai chhota, par 20 saal mein woh ₹1.2 Lakh ban jaata hai.

Quick calculation shortcut

Agar aapko rough estimate chahiye, toh ek simple thumb rule hai: har ₹1 Lakh ke loan par 8–9% rate aur 5 saal tenure mein roughly ₹2,000–₹2,100 EMI aati hai. Toh ₹5 Lakh loan = roughly ₹10,000–10,500 EMI per month.

Final baat

EMI ko samajhna koi rocket science nahi hai — bas ek baar formula ki logic pakad lo. Yaad rakho: loan sirf tab lena chahiye jab aap EMI comfortably afford kar sako aur total interest bhi apni financial planning mein fit ho. Ek samajhdaar borrower wahi hota hai jo loan lene se pehle calculator chalata hai, na lene ke baad pachtata hai.

📌 Rule of thumb Apni monthly income ka 40% se zyada kabhi EMI mein mat lagao. Matlab ₹50,000 salary hai toh ₹20,000 se zyada ki total EMI mat lo — sabhi loans milaakar.
